About

I am a photographer based in Portland, Oregon. For the past eight years, I have traveled across the Pacific Northwest capturing the quiet beauty of forests, coastlines, and mountain ridges at dawn.

My work has been featured in National Geographic, Outdoor Magazine, and several independent galleries in Seattle and San Francisco. I believe the best photographs are the ones that make you pause and breathe.
